DOOMNUT

Privacy Policy — Effective March 2026

1. Introduction

DOOMNUT is a social deduction party game developed by Pierre-Louis Cargnello. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use the DOOMNUT mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

DOOMNUT does not collect any personal information directly. The app does not require account creation, login, email address, or any form of registration.

All game data (player names, scores, settings, and game history) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdMob — Used to display advertisements. Google may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — Used to manage in-app purchases. RevenueCat may process transaction data through the Google Play Store. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Store — Handles app distribution and in-app purchase transactions. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

DOOMNUT displays ads through Google AdMob. You may see personalized ads based on your interests, or non-personalized ads depending on your device settings and consent choices. You can manage your ad preferences in your device's settings.

5. In-App Purchases

DOOMNUT offers optional in-app purchases to unlock additional word packs. These transactions are processed entirely through the Google Play Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

6. Children's Privacy

DOOMNUT is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect any personal data, no special provisions are required.

7. Data Retention

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data will permanently delete all locally stored information.

8. Your Rights

As we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete on our end. For data collected by third-party services (Google, RevenueCat), please refer to their respective privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
