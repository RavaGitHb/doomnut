About the Mission

Doomnut is a social deduction party game for 4 to 8 players, designed to play together on a single device.

One Officer holds a secret word. The Crew tries to guess it through yes/no questions. But hidden among them, a Virus and a Parasite are pulling the mission in their own direction.

Read the Game Rules

The Crew

Officer
Knows the secret word and sets the wager. Answers only YES or NO.
Crew
Hunts the secret word with sharp, well-placed questions.
Virus
Knows the word too, but plays from the shadows to mislead the Crew.
ParasiteOPTIONAL
Got a different word. Wants their word guessed instead of the Officer's.

Field Manual

How many players are needed?
Doomnut is designed for 4 to 8 players, all on a single device.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. The whole game runs offline. Internet is only used for optional features like ads and in-app purchases.
What languages are available?
English and French. The app follows your device language by default and can be changed in Settings.
